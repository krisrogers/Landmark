Sun path alignment test

Throwaway diagnostic. It draws where the sun travels on the winter and summer solstices and the equinox over your live camera view, using your phone's compass and tilt. It checks whether the drawn paths line up with reality before we build light assessment on top.

  1. Tap Start and allow camera, location and motion sensors.
  2. Hold the phone upright (portrait), away from cars, fences and other metal.
  3. On a sunny day, put the real sun in the centre crosshair (look at the screen, not the sun) and tap Calibrate. That corrects the compass error.
  4. Check the yellow sun now marker stays on the real sun as you move the phone so the sun sits off-centre. If it drifts toward the edges, adjust FOV until it stays on.
  5. Sweep across the northern sky: the cyan line is where the midwinter sun travels.
